2. Categories of data processed, purposes and legal basis

2.1. Technical usage data

If you use the website for information purposes only, that is, if you do not register or otherwise send us information, we only collect the data that your browser transmits to our server. When you view our website, we collect the following data:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Content of the request (specific page)
  • Access status/HTTP status code
  • Website from which the request originates (“referrer”)
  • Information about the operating system and browser software
  • Preferred language setting and screen resolution

We process this data for the following purposes:

  • ensuring a smooth connection to the website,
  • ensuring convenient use of our website,
  • evaluating system security and stability.

This data may relate to you personally only via the IP address. Depending on how you access the internet, the IP address may be completely anonymous, or your internet service provider may store the assignment of an IP address to you. In that case, combining the above data with that of your internet service provider could make it possible to link the above data to you. However, we will only initiate this if it is warranted in an individual case for the purpose of legal enforcement, and we will otherwise only cooperate in it if ordered to do so by a public authority or court in an individual case.

In addition, we process this data to compile aggregated anonymous usage statistics that help us improve our website.

The data is stored until the end of the calendar month following your use and is then deleted within two working days (unless it is required in an individual case for the purpose of legal enforcement).

Insofar as the data processing serves to provide the website, the legal basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(1)(b) GDPR); otherwise, it is a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(1)(f) GDPR). Our legitimate interest follows from the purposes of data collection listed above. Under no circumstances do we use the collected data to draw conclusions about you as a person.

2.2. Contact by email or contact form

If you have any questions, you can contact us via a form provided on the website or by email. A valid email address is required so that we know who the enquiry is from and can answer it.

Data is processed for the purpose of contacting us in order to perform a contract (Art. 6(1)(1)(b) GDPR). The personal data collected when you contact us is deleted once your enquiry has been dealt with or, if it constitutes “business letters” within the meaning of Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code (AO), it is blocked and stored for a period of 6 years to fulfil statutory tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(1)(c) GDPR).

The data is processed on behalf of the provider by MailerSend, Inc., 228 Park Ave S, PMB 54955, New York, NY 10003-1502, USA. The data is stored in data centres in the European Union. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is done on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which MailerSend, Inc. is certified. You can find privacy information here: https://www.mailersend.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Use of cookies

This website currently only uses technically necessary cookies and similar technologies, insofar as this is required to operate the website.

Analytics, tracking or marketing cookies are only used if this is described in this privacy policy and, where required, consent has been obtained.

4. Withdrawal of consent and objection

4.1. Withdrawal of consent

If you have given consent to the processing of your data and/or to receiving marketing emails, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. To do so, notify the provider named in section 1 above or its data protection officer (for example by email).

4.2. Objection

Insofar as we base the processing of your personal data on a legitimate interest (legal basis Art. 6(1)(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to the processing. To do so, notify the provider named in section 1 above or its data protection officer (for example by email). If you object, we ask you to explain why your interest outweighs ours. In the event of your objection, we will review the situation and either stop or adjust the data processing, or show you our overriding interest on the basis of which we will continue the processing.

5. Your rights

You are entitled to request from us:

  • access to your personal data (right of access) pursuant to Art. 15 GDPR;
  • rectification of any inaccurate personal data pursuant to Art. 16 GDPR;
  • erasure of your personal data in certain cases pursuant to Art. 17 GDPR;
  • restriction of processing of your personal data while a request for rectification or erasure is being reviewed, pursuant to Art. 18 GDPR;
  • a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format in order to transfer it to another controller (data portability) pursuant to Art. 20 GDPR.

If you wish to exercise these rights, notify the provider named in section 1 above (for example by email).

6. Complaints

If you wish to complain about the data processing, notify the provider named in section 1 above or its data protection officer (for example by email). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority. This is the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen). Alternatively, you can lodge your complaint with any other data protection supervisory authority within the European Economic Area, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

7. Currency and changes to this privacy policy

This privacy information is currently valid and was last updated on 3 June 2026.
