Circlaris

Circlaris is web-based software (B2B SaaS) for professional dealers who buy, service, manage, list and sell pre-owned luxury watches, jewellery and gold.

Circlaris covers the path of a watch from buying through service, inventory and listing to sale in one system. The software has been in daily use with watch and jewellery dealers since 2022 and is developed and operated by CHH Ventures GmbH.

Circlaris is not a dealer and not a marketplace. Circlaris does not buy or sell watches, jewellery or gold itself; dealers use it as a working tool.

Features of Circlaris

Circlaris takes each item through the stations of the trade. Every station works with the same item data.

Buying

Buying requests with photos and asking price are recorded on a phone or a computer. After inspection and a provenance check, Circlaris creates the purchase agreement, which is signed digitally. Insured collection can be arranged on request. Outright purchase and consignment are handled separately.

Service

Servicing and polishing in the dealer’s own workshop or by an external watchmaker are tracked on the watch with status, due date and cost. Every job is recorded in the service history.

Inventory

Every item has an item number and a storage location. Item details, product attributes, purchase price, service work and accessories are kept in one place. Stocktaking is done on your phone.

Listing

Circlaris creates listings from item data and photos and publishes them on the connected marketplaces and in the dealer’s own shop. The commission for each marketplace is factored in.

Sale

A sale in the shop, at a trade fair or via a marketplace generates the invoice and delivery note, including as an e-invoice. Circlaris assigns the VAT treatment, such as the VAT margin scheme, standard taxation, EU tax rates or exports outside the EU. Shipping labels and tracking are connected, and the export for the accountant takes one click.

Gold and precious metals

An add-on module manages scrap gold and precious metals with a melt pool and material tracking.

Reporting

Circlaris reports on revenue, gross profit, margin and purchase volume.

Users of Circlaris

Circlaris is aimed exclusively at business customers, not at private consumers. Typical users are:

  • Dealers in pre-owned watches
  • Jewellers and jewellery dealers
  • Gold and precious metals dealers
  • Shops that buy pre-owned goods and sell them on marketplaces

Most businesses that use Circlaris work with one to three people.

Distinguishing Circlaris

Circlaris is software for dealers. Circlaris is not a watch or jewellery shop, not a gold buyer, not a public marketplace and not a valuation service. Anyone who wants to sell or buy a watch deals with a dealer; Circlaris itself does not conclude purchase or sale contracts with private individuals.

The name Circlaris refers to the software of CHH Ventures GmbH at circlaris.com. Circlaris is not to be confused with similarly spelled names such as “Circularis” or with companies from the circular economy.

FAQ about Circlaris

What is Circlaris?

Circlaris is web-based software for professional dealers in pre-owned watches, jewellery and gold. It brings buying, service, inventory, listing and sale together in one system.

Who operates Circlaris?

Circlaris is developed and operated by CHH Ventures GmbH. The company has its registered seat in Berlin and its business address in Cologne; its Managing Director is Dr. Thomas Hansmann.

Does Circlaris buy or sell watches itself?

No. Circlaris is software. Buying and selling are carried out by the dealers who use Circlaris.

Where does Circlaris store data?

Data is stored in data centres in Germany, operated by Hetzner. A data processing agreement under the GDPR is concluded with the contract.

Is Circlaris the same as Circularis?

No. Circlaris is the software of CHH Ventures GmbH at circlaris.com and has no connection with similarly spelled names such as “Circularis”.
