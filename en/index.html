Good to know

What does Circlaris cost?

We will tell you the price during the demo, depending on your business.

I also deal in jewellery and gold. Is that possible?

Buying jewellery is part of the buying process. For gold and precious metals there is an add-on module with melt pool and material tracking.

What about tax and accounting?

VAT margin scheme or standard taxation, EU tax rates and exports outside the EU: Circlaris assigns the correct VAT treatment automatically. Your accountant receives the export with one click.

I work on my own. Is Circlaris too big for me?

Most dealers who use Circlaris work with one to three people. You can start buying on the first day, and integrations are added when you need them.

Do I have to give up my marketplaces?

No. Circlaris is connected to exactly the channels on which watch dealers sell: eBay, Chrono24, Wristler, Kleinanzeigen and your online shop on Shopware or Shopify. You manage everything in one place.

Can I use Circlaris on a mobile device?

Yes. Circlaris runs in the browser, including on your phone. You can create a buying request with photos, asking price and ID while on the move, for example at a trade fair, and the agreement is signed on the spot.
